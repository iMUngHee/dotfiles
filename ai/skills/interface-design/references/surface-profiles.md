# Surface Profiles

Read the shared quality floor plus only the primary profile that matches the approved experience. Profiles supply defaults where the contract is silent; they never override an approved contract or accessibility minimum.

## Dashboard / Admin / Operations

- Job: scan, compare, filter, recover, and repeat actions quickly.
- Density: compact; rows typically 32-40px, desktop toolbar controls 32-36px, panels 16-24px padding.
- Structure: sidebars, tables, filters, segmented controls, drawers, and dialogs before heroes or card grids.
- Type: body 13-14px, labels 11-12px, page title 18-24px; IDs and numbers may use mono/tabular figures.
- Motion: none for repeated row/filter/keyboard actions; brief direct feedback only.
- Components: sticky/sortable/filterable tables where useful, overflow and bulk states, empty/error rows, and volume-aware pagination or virtualization.
- Avoid: marketing copy, decorative whitespace, card-only work queues, color-only priority, and repeated-action animation.

## Form-Heavy Flow

- Job: complete data entry with confidence, recovery, and minimal rework.
- Density: moderate; group fields into readable 480-720px columns with 16-24px vertical rhythm.
- Structure: explicit labels, progressive sections for long flows, stable action placement, and review/save visibility.
- Type: labels 13-14px, helper/error 12-13px, section titles 16-20px.
- Motion: brief validation or section feedback; none while typing.
- Components: label, helper, invalid, disabled, loading, readonly, required/optional, dirty, and saved states as applicable.
- Avoid: placeholder-only labels, hidden errors, unexplained disabled submit, irreversible dead ends, and avoidable late validation.

## Data Visualization

- Job: compare, detect patterns, and trust the data.
- Density: preserve definitions, labels, legends, and annotations before decorative whitespace.
- Structure: metric definition, timeframe, source/update status, axes, legend, and empty/error state belong to the chart.
- Type: labels 11-13px, annotations 12-14px, titles 16-20px, tabular figures for numbers.
- Color: categorical palettes differ in hue and luminance; sequential palettes use ordered lightness; never color-only.
- Motion: only when it preserves object constancy through filtering or time changes.
- Avoid: unlabelled axes, decorative gradients, 3D effects, cropped legends, and unlabeled totals.

## Landing / Marketing

- Job: understand the offer, trust it, and choose the next action.
- Density: low to moderate; the first viewport carries product/brand signal, value, proof or product evidence, and one primary action.
- Structure: derive a coherent macrostructure from the offer and audience; do not default to interchangeable SaaS section stacks.
- Type: hero 40-64px desktop and 32-44px mobile; supporting copy 16-20px; section headings 24-40px.
- Motion: low-frequency and brand/product-serving; reduced motion preserves content.
- Components: navigation, CTA, product media, proof, objections, pricing/next step, and responsive states as applicable.
- Avoid: vague claims, stock-like visuals, hero copy trapped in generic cards, hidden product evidence, and competing CTAs.

## Content / Editorial

- Job: consume, evaluate, and navigate content.
- Density: reading-first; main measure 60-80 characters, paragraph spacing 16-24px, line height 1.55-1.75.
- Structure: article body, useful table of contents, media/captions, and related navigation driven by content.
- Type: body 16-18px, h1 36-56px desktop, h2 24-36px, captions 12-14px.
- Motion: minimal and never interrupting reading flow.
- Components: headings, links, media, code, callouts, footnotes, and search/no-result states when applicable.
- Avoid: dashboard density, decorative widgets, low-contrast text, broken measure, and reading-interrupting animation.

## Game / Expressive Tool

- Job: understand state, feel feedback, and enjoy interaction.
- Density: state-first; primary state remains visible and secondary controls may collapse.
- Structure: the play/work area dominates; HUD, inventory, score, timers, and mode controls stay predictable.
- Type: counters and labels remain legible under motion; expressive display type stays out of high-frequency state reading.
- Motion: direct feedback may be richer but controls stay responsive; celebration is low-frequency and reducible.
- Components: start, pause, reset, mode, score/state, invalid action, and reduced-motion variants.
- Avoid: ambiguous state, hidden controls, inaccessible key actions, repeated-input animation, and effects that cover important state.

## Product App / Workflow

- Job: complete recurring tasks, retain context, and recover safely.
- Density: moderate; work-list rows 36-44px, panel padding 16-24px, control gaps 8-16px.
- Structure: persistent navigation, clear current object, predictable detail panels, bounded dialogs, and recoverable inline editing.
- Type: body 14-16px, labels 12-13px, page title 22-32px, section title 16-20px.
- Motion: brief navigation/detail feedback; avoid animation on repeated power-user flows.
- Components: nav, toolbar, list/table, detail, form, dialog, toast, empty/loading/error/conflict, and undo/recovery.
- Avoid: decorative layout that slows repetition, hidden state changes, unrecoverable actions, and feature cards replacing workflows.

## Presentation / Deck

- Job: present one idea per slide, legibly at distance, with speaker support.
- Canvas: fixed 16:9 by default; scale the frame rather than applying web breakpoints.
- Density: one primary idea, generous margins, and consistent safe area.
- Structure: stable master, section dividers, title plus one supporting visual or point block, presenter notes when supported.
- Type: title 40-72pt-equivalent, body 24-32pt-equivalent, never below about 20pt-equivalent.
- Color: projection-safe contrast and one reserved emphasis accent.
- Motion: one consistent advance; builds only to pace a single idea.
- Avoid: dense paragraphs, scroll assumptions, tiny type, decorative per-element animation, and document-like slides.
